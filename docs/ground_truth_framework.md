# Agni-Netra Ground Truth Acquisition Framework

## 1. Core Principle
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Weak label ≠ ground truth.**
> Verified ground truth is independent evidence and is strictly NOT generated by Agni-Netra's own prediction pipeline. Models (B1 anomaly, B2 classifiers, OSM heuristics) cannot elevate an event to a verified status.

## 2. Label Tiers

- **GOLD**: Independently verified using strong external evidence (e.g., high-res satellite imagery, government incident reports, confirmed news articles).
- **SILVER**: Multiple independent evidence sources exist but leave room for minor doubt, or strong evidence that slightly lacks temporal precision.
- **BRONZE**: Existing Agni-Netra heuristic/weak labels (e.g., Phase 3/5 OSM intersection logic). These are hypotheses, not facts.
- **UNKNOWN**: Insufficient evidence to make a confident classification.
- **DISPUTED**: Expert reviewers fundamentally disagree on the classification despite reviewing the same evidence.

## 3. Independence Rule
A **GOLD** or **SILVER** label must NEVER be established solely from:
- The original OSM proxy heuristics
- The existing BRONZE weak-label rules
- The B2 model predictions
- The B1 anomaly scores
- FIRMS thermal intensity alone
- Sentinel-2 automated NDVI/NBR extraction alone (human review of the optical scene is required)

## 4. Blind Review Mechanism
To scientifically measure model-induced bias, a random subset of candidates (e.g., 20%) will undergo **Blind Review**.
- **Blind Reviewers** will receive: Date, location, ID, and raw external sources.
- **Blind Reviewers** will NOT receive: The B1 Anomaly score, the BRONZE weak label, or any Agni-Netra predicted class.
- Comparing blind reviews against model-assisted reviews will quantify human confirmation bias.

## 5. Inter-Rater Agreement
Events reviewed by multiple experts must have their agreement tracked. Disagreements must NOT be resolved by silent majority vote. Disagreements result in a `DISPUTED` status, preserving the ambiguity as a valuable data point. Cohen's Kappa will be calculated for overlapping expert subsets.
